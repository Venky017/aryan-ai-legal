# Aryan AI — Data Deletion Instructions

**Last updated:** 22 October 2026

Aryan AI is a personal AI assistant project developed by Venkey.

## How to Request Data Deletion

If you have interacted with Aryan AI and would like your personal data deleted, you can submit a deletion request using the contact email provided in the Aryan AI Privacy Policy.

Please include:
- A description of the data you want deleted.
- The phone number or account identifier associated with your interaction, if applicable.

Do not include passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive credentials.

## What Happens Next

We will review your request, verify account ownership where necessary, and delete applicable personal data from systems under our control, subject to legal or security retention requirements.

We aim to respond to deletion requests within 30 days.

## Third-Party Services

Data held independently by third-party services, including Meta WhatsApp and Zerodha, may be subject to their own data deletion policies.

## Contact

For data deletion requests, please use the contact details published in the Aryan AI Privacy Policy.
