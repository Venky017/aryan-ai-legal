Aryan AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. Introduction

Aryan AI is a personal AI assistant project developed by Venkey, supporting productivity, automation, market analysis, trading notifications and user-authorized actions.

2. Information We Process

Aryan AI may process WhatsApp phone numbers, messages, delivery information, commands, preferences, trading proposals, approval decisions and technical diagnostic information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide notifications, process authorized commands, record decisions, maintain security and troubleshoot the service.

4. Third-Party Services

Aryan AI may integrate with Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and Zerodha, which process information under their respective policies.

5. Security and Retention

Aryan AI uses applicable security controls. Information is retained as necessary for operational, legal and security purposes.

6. Data Sharing

Aryan AI does not sell personal information to advertisers. Information may be shared with necessary service providers or where required by law.

7. Data Deletion and User Rights

Users may request access, correction or deletion of their personal information. Requests are reviewed subject to applicable legal and technical requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Aryan AI is not designed for children under 18.

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as Aryan AI develops.

10. Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, contact the Aryan AI project operator using the contact details in the official Meta app listing.
