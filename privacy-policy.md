# Aryan AI — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 22 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

Aryan AI is a personal AI assistant project developed by Venkey. It is designed to support personal productivity, automation, market analysis, trading notifications, and user-authorized actions.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when interacting with Aryan AI, including through the WhatsApp Business Platform.

## 2. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, Aryan AI may process:

- WhatsApp phone numbers, incoming messages, and message delivery information.
- Commands, responses, and user-provided preferences.
- Trading proposals, approval or rejection responses, and related transaction references.
- Technical information required to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Aryan AI is not intended to request passwords, banking PINs, or other sensitive authentication credentials through WhatsApp.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to:

- Send notifications and respond to user requests.
- Receive and interpret authorized WhatsApp commands.
- Present trading opportunities and record user decisions.
- Maintain security, diagnose errors, and improve reliability.

Trading actions are subject to separate authorization and safety controls. Receiving a WhatsApp message does not, by itself, authorize an unrestricted financial transaction.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Aryan AI may integrate with third-party services, including Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and Zerodha.

Information sent through these services may also be processed under their respective privacy policies and applicable terms.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Aryan AI uses application-level security measures, including access controls and webhook signature verification where implemented.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

Message records and diagnostic information may be stored where the corresponding application features are enabled. Storage and retention practices may change as the project develops.

## 6. Data Sharing

Aryan AI does not sell personal information to advertisers.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate requested features, or when required by applicable law.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Personal information is retained only as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to operational, legal, and security requirements.

Users may request access to or deletion of personal information by contacting the project operator.

Deletion requests will be reviewed, and applicable information will be deleted or anonymized where technically feasible and legally permitted.

## 8. User Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Aryan AI is not designed for use by children under 18.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Aryan AI's features or data-processing practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

## 11. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Aryan AI project operator through the contact information published in the application's official Meta listing.
